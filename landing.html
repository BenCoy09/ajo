Form the circle

Invite people you trust, pick the amount and how often to pay. The payout order is set before anyone pays.

Pay each round

Everyone adds their share. The pot sits in the contract, not in one person's account.

Collect your turn

When it's your round, the full pot goes to you. Then the circle moves on to the next member.

Built so the circle can't be broken by one person.

No one holds the money. Contributions go into a smart contract and are released by the rules you agreed on.

Everyone can see the order. Whose turn it is, who has paid and what is left are visible to the whole circle.

Fast and cheap on Monad. Paying in small amounts every week stays worth it when fees are tiny.
